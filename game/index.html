HOW TO PLAY
A coastal city of palms, neon and old debts. Mara Voss drives a taxi by day — and by night she runs jobs for Dax: stolen cars, hot packages, one last bank score.
WASD / Arrows — move & drive  ·  E — enter car / interact  ·  Q / E — orbit camera
Space / Click — shoot  ·  1–4 — weapons  ·  Shift — brake  ·  R — radio
G — Udin's Garage menu  ·  P — pause & save  ·  Drag mouse — look around
Follow the gold marker to run Dax's jobs, earn Respect, and become a legend of the shore.
